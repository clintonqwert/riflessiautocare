# Riflessi Auto Care charter

## Purpose

Riflessi Auto Care is a premium, appointment-only Metro Vancouver auto-detailing site designed to convert visitors into detail bookings and establish trust before a customer contacts the business.

## Current product shape

- Static, server-first Next.js marketing and lead-generation site.
- Primary conversion goal: a completed detail-booking inquiry.
- The site is Version 1 of a reusable automotive service website framework.

## Brand guardrails

- Present the business honestly; claims must trace to the content facts modules.
- Do not imply an indoor facility or use stock work as evidence of completed client work.
- The cinematic experience must enhance, never block, the core content or booking journey.
